# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no build step, no npm at runtime. Delivered two ways: a folder the owner drags into Hostinger `public_html` (with `.htaccess` and cache-busting query strings), and a published Artifact link for immediate viewing. Confirmed by the user.

## Users

Homeowners and small landlords in the Maresme and La Selva (Tordera, Blanes, Lloret, Malgrat, Pineda, Calella, Mataró) and more broadly Barcelona and Girona provinces, planning a renovation of a bathroom, kitchen or whole flat, or a small new build. They typically arrive from a Google search or a referral, often on a phone, comparing two or three local companies. Their job: decide whether this company is trustworthy enough to invite for a free visit and quote.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Reformas F.S Fonseca, a small renovation and installations company based in Tordera (Barcelona). Success means a visitor requests a visit or quote by phone, WhatsApp or the contact form.

## Positioning

A small company on purpose: the person who quotes the job is the same one who runs it and answers for it. Nine years in the trade, free visit and a written, itemised quote before any work starts.

## Operating Context

The sales conversation happens offline: visit to measure, written quote with itemised line items, then execution. The website's only job is to earn that first contact. Visitors frequently check on a phone while at home looking at the room they want to change.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services confirmed from the company's public habitissimo listing: bathroom renovation, whole-home renovation, house construction / new build, air conditioning, plasterboard (pladur / yeso laminado), masonry (albañilería), painting and decoration.
- Service provinces: Barcelona and Girona. Base: Mediterrany, 08490 Tordera.
- The contact form has no backend; it opens WhatsApp with the message pre-written.
- **Prices:** decision delegated to Claude by the user. No prices or price ranges are published, because the owner has not validated any figures. Instead of package blocks, the page states what every job includes (protection, debris removal, permits, schedule, final cleaning, snagging) and offers a picker of job types that jumps to the contact form.
- Phone and WhatsApp are the real ones (613 76 64 76). No email address exists, so the contact form opens WhatsApp with the message pre-written instead of a mail client. The domain is not confirmed, so no canonical URL is published.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Reformas F.S Fonseca" (short form "F.S Fonseca").
- Logo mark created in a previous iteration: a builder's level triangle with a centred bubble. May be reused or refined.
- The user explicitly asked for the new site to resemble the look and animations of https://centroebm.com/ (visual reference, adapted to renovation). This is a binding visual reference for this build.
- The user's standing preference across projects: formal, sober, simple; photography carries the visual weight; animation present but discreet.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before persuasion: every claim on the page must be verifiable from the evidence above.
2. One interlocutor is the differentiator; say it plainly and early.
3. Contact must be one tap away on a phone at every point of the page.
4. Placeholders are centralised and obvious to the owner, never presented as real data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience includes older homeowners: body text at least 17px, strong contrast on text over photographs, generous tap targets, and motion that never hides content or blocks reading.
